What is Fast Fashion?

Fast fashion is clothing that is produced quickly and cheaply to follow the latest trends. This can cause large amounts of waste and harm the environment.

Sustainable Tips

Making sustainable clothing choices can help reduce waste. Buying clothes that last longer, repairing damaged clothing and shopping at thrift stores are simple ways to become more sustainable.
